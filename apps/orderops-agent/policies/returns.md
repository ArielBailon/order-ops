# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. The 30 days start on the day the carrier marks the order as delivered, not the day you placed it.

## Eligible items

Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories and manuals. Clearance items marked "final sale" cannot be returned. Gift cards and digital downloads are not returnable.

## How to return

Start a return from your order page or contact support with your order number. We email a prepaid return label within one business day. Pack the item securely and drop it off with the carrier named on the label.

## Refunds

Once the returned item arrives at our warehouse and passes inspection, we refund the original payment method within 5 business days. Original shipping fees are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item. Return shipping is free for damaged or wrong items and costs 6.00 USD for any other reason, deducted from the refund.

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order.
