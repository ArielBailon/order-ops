# Shipping Policy

## Shipping options

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs 4.99 USD. Orders over 50 USD ship free with standard shipping. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 12.99 USD.

## Processing time

Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after that time or on weekends ship the next business day.

## Tracking

You receive a tracking number by email when your order ships. You can also see the status and tracking number on your order page.

## Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states and to Canada. We do not ship to PO boxes with express shipping. International orders outside the US and Canada are not available.

## Delays and lost packages

If your tracking has not updated for 5 business days, contact support and we will open a carrier investigation. If a package is confirmed lost, we send a replacement or refund the order in full.
