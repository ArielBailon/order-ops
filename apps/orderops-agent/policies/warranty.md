# Warranty Policy

## Coverage

Every product we sell includes a 12-month limited warranty from the delivery date. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal household use.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover damage from drops, spills, or misuse, normal wear such as scratches or fading, or products modified or repaired by anyone other than us.

## Making a claim

Contact support with your order number and a short description or photo of the problem. We review claims within 3 business days. Approved claims are repaired, replaced, or refunded at our choice.

## Extended warranty

There is no paid extended warranty option. The 12-month warranty cannot be extended.
